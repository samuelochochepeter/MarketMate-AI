02 / YOUR DATA

Bring your own CSV files

Use the supplied column names. Dates follow YYYY-MM-DD. Sales records should describe one product with one row per day.

Files stay in this running local session. The app does not save uploaded records. No customer names, phone numbers or account credentials are needed.

Cleaning notes and definitions

promo: 0 = no promotion, 1 = promotion. Revenue is units × unit price, before costs. Click-to-lead conversion uses reported leads ÷ clicks. Cost per lead uses social spend ÷ leads. It is undefined when leads are zero.

03 / DEMAND LAB

A small model with a measurable baseline

A random forest estimates next-day units from weekday, price, promotion and ad spend. The most recent 20% of daily records form the test set.

Next-day scenario

How to read the estimate

MAE is the average absolute error in units. Lower is better. The baseline always predicts the mean of the training period.

The model demonstrates a workflow on synthetic data. It does not prove real-world accuracy or the causal effect of changing price or ad spend.

After evaluation, the model refits on all supplied history for the next-day scenario. At least 40 daily records are required.
