# Thirty-second Reel storyboard

Fictional Sunrise Bakes campaign. Planned script, not evidence of an actual published video. Film only a real process you can verify and have permission to show.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover or caption | Accessibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 seconds | Close shot of a loaf | What do you look for in your daily loaf? | Large readable captions |
| 2–8 seconds | Show the product and packaging | Here is a closer look at our bread. | Describe the product without invented claims |
| 8–25 seconds | Brief genuine preparation footage and serving scene | Ask us about availability, collection and ingredients. We will confirm the details before you order. | Correct captions and sufficient colour contrast |
| 25–30 seconds | Clear message prompt | Send us a message with your question. | One clear CTA, no tiny text |

Hook B for a later test: What would you like to know before ordering bread? Keep the remaining content constant. Avoid claiming that an organic comparison isolates a causal effect.
